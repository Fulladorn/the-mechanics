# Audio credits

All recorded samples in this folder are from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released
under CC0 1.0 Universal — see `LICENSE-kenney.txt`.

- Impact Sounds — footsteps and impacts (`foot_*`, `imp_*`)
- RPG Audio — doors, cloth, metal handling (`door*`, `creak*`, `cloth*`, `metal*`, `belt*`, …)
- Interface Sounds — menus and HUD (`ui_*`)

The engine, ambience, music and Dispatch's radio voice are synthesised at
runtime (`src/client/audio/mixer.ts`).
